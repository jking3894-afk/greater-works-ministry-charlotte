About Us

Called to Greater Works

We exist to glorify God and help people grow into the purpose He has placed on their lives.

Greater Works Ministry of Charlotte is a welcoming community of believers committed to the Gospel of Jesus Christ, discipleship, spiritual growth, and service. We believe the Holy Spirit empowers ordinary people to accomplish extraordinary works for God’s glory.

Our Mission

To glorify God by preaching the Gospel of Jesus Christ, making disciples, equipping believers for ministry, and transforming lives through the power of the Holy Spirit, so that we may do the greater works Christ has called us to accomplish.

Our Vision

“Greater People, Doing Greater Works, for the Glory of God.”

Meet Our Pastor

Pastor Kimberly Hines

Pastor Kimberly Hines is a devoted servant of Jesus Christ, a wife, mother, and grandmother, and the founder and pastor of Greater Works Ministry. She is the wife of Steven Hines and considers her family one of God’s greatest blessings.

With a heart for people and a passion for the Word of God, Pastor Kimberly is committed to helping individuals discover their identity in Christ, grow in their faith, and walk boldly in the purpose God has placed within them. Her ministry is rooted in prayer, biblical teaching, discipleship, and a desire to see lives transformed through the power and presence of God.

For more than a decade, Pastor Kimberly has carried a burden to reach people beyond the walls of the church and to equip believers to fulfill the call of God on their lives. She believes that every believer has a purpose and that God desires to use ordinary people to accomplish extraordinary things for His glory.

Her life and ministry are inspired by the words of Jesus in John 14:12:

“He that believeth on me, the works that I do shall he do also; and greater works than these shall he do.”

Pastor Kimberly’s heart and vision are summed up in the mission of Greater Works Ministry:

Greater People. Doing Greater Works. For the Glory of God.
